# PALM: itch.io page

## Short description
Blast it, or let it pass.

## Description (paste into the itch editor)

**PALM** is a split-second reaction game where one palm stands between your friends and whatever comes through the panel.

A masked guardian holds up a hand. Faces burst into a manga panel, one beat at a time. You get one call per face: **BLAST** it, or **LET IT PASS**. Three onlookers stand behind you, and they are your lives.

There is no aim, no combo string and no loadout. Only **read, decide, act**.

## 👁️ Read the Face
- **Aura:** The first read. Red is a threat, gold is a friend.
- **Eyes:** The second read. Slit pupils mean a threat. The aura can lie; the eyes never do.
- **Violet:** A special. Something about this one changes the timing. Watch the eyes.

## 🎭 Five Arrivals
- **Wanderer:** Pops in and waits for your verdict.
- **Lurker:** Hides in the shadow for a jab or a long hold. Sometimes nothing comes out at all.
- **Sleeper:** Eyes shut. Wait until they open, then read them.
- **Twins:** Two faces, one verdict. Wait for both, then blast if either is a threat.
- **Turncoat:** Smiles at first. Some of them turn, early or late.

## ⚡ Strikes & Stakes
- **Strike:** A wrong blast, a threat let through, or a move made too early. Every mistake is readable on screen the moment you make it.
- **Onlookers:** Let a threat through and one gets knocked out. Make any other mistake and one flees.
- **The Slump:** Lose all three and the guardian falls.
- **The Snap:** Blast the instant the truth shows itself for a bigger hit and a bonus.

## ⚙️ Features & Depth
- **The Hakai:** An instant violet blast with hit-stop, a whiteout and a deep boom. No charge, no beam.
- **Janky Hand-Drawn Faces:** Procedural faces that are never quite the same twice.
- **Fair by Design:** No type looks like another with a different rule. If you lost the call, you can see why.
- **Learn, Then Mix:** Each new arrival debuts three in a row, then joins the crowd. Once you've met them all, the order is dealt fresh each run.
- **Original Soundtrack:** *Introspecs* by Mikey Lambo, looping seamlessly underneath.
- **Global Leaderboard:** Post your score and share your game-over frame.
- **Instant Restarts & Zero Bloat:** A run takes a few minutes. Press any button to go again.

## 🎮 Controls
**Desktop**
- **Blast:** Space / → / D
- **Let Pass:** ← / ↓ / A / S
- **Pause:** P / Esc

**Mobile**
- **Blast / Let Pass:** Tap the on-screen buttons, or swipe

*Sound is recommended. The music and hits carry a lot of the feel.*
