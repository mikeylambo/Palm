## Description

**A masked guardian holds up a palm. Faces burst into the panel. You have a split second.**

BLAST the threats. LET the friends PASS. Three onlookers stand behind you, and they're your lives.

PALM is a raw, high-energy reflex game in the spirit of WarioWare. Every call takes a single press. Getting it right takes a lot more.

**Read fast**
- Aura first: red is a threat, gold is a friend.
- Eyes second: slit pupils never lie. The aura sometimes does.

**Then they get clever**
- **The Lurker** waits in the shadow. Sometimes nothing comes out at all.
- **The Sleeper** keeps their eyes shut. Wait until they open.
- **The Twins** are two faces with one verdict. Wait for both.
- **The Turncoat** smiles at first. Some of them turn.

Move too early and it's a STRIKE. Let a threat through and an onlooker gets knocked out. Three strikes and the guardian falls.

**Features**
- One button to blast and one to pass. Keyboard, mouse or touch.
- Hand-drawn, intentionally janky faces that are never quite the same twice.
- Every mistake is readable on screen. It's never a cheap shot.
- An original soundtrack, *Introspecs*, by Mikey Lambo.
- A global leaderboard.

**Controls**
- BLAST: Space / → / D (or tap BLAST)
- LET PASS: ← / ↓ / A / S (or tap LET PASS)
- Pause: P / Esc

Made by Mikey Lambo.
